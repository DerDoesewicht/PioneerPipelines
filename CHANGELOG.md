# Pioneer Pipelines 1.1.0

- Added three fluid buffers supporting up to 1,200 m³/min per connection: small, industrial and XL with 10,000 m³ storage.
- Added dedicated buffer icons for the build menu and milestone rewards.
- Added Pioneer Oil Extractor 1200 and Pioneer Resource Well Extractor 1200.
- Both new extractors produce 240 m³/min on normal sources at 100% clock and reach 1,200 m³/min on pure sources at 250%.
- Oil extractor: 2× vanilla extraction, base power consumption and construction materials.
- Resource well extractor: 4× vanilla extraction and construction materials, using the standard pressurizer for activation and clock control. Pressurizer power consumption is unchanged.
- Added the new buildings to Advanced Fluid Transport, including recipe migration for eligible existing saves.
- Kept the existing water extractor at 1,200 m³/min at 100% clock.
- Kept the internal test sink disabled for normal gameplay.
- Fixed extractor-generator helper name collisions in unity builds and logging block braces in content diagnostics.

## Known limitation

The standard Resource Well Pressurizer potential display does not reflect the enhanced Pioneer extractor output. Check the individual Pioneer extractor's production and flow display.
